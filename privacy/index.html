Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains how PaperwisePlanners ("we", "us") handles information, both on this website and in the PaperwisePlanners Pin Publisher, a private tool that publishes pins about our own products to our own Pinterest account.

Who we are

PaperwisePlanners is a small digital-products shop run by one person in Israel, selling printable and digital planners on Etsy at etsy.com/shop/PaperwisePlanners. You can contact us through Etsy messages on that page.

This website

This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or sign-up forms, and we do not collect personal information through it. GitHub, as the host, may log technical data such as IP addresses for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Purchases

All purchases, payments and downloads happen on Etsy. Etsy shares with us only what it needs to fulfil an order (for example, your Etsy username and order details). We use this only to fulfil the order and answer your messages, never for marketing lists, and we do not sell or share it. Etsy's own handling of your data is described in the Etsy Privacy Policy.

PaperwisePlanners Pin Publisher (Pinterest app)

The Pin Publisher is used only by the shop owner, for the shop's own Pinterest business account. It is not offered to other Pinterest users.

  • What it accesses: after the account owner signs in with Pinterest and approves access (Pinterest OAuth), the app receives an access token with the scopes user_accounts:read, boards:read, boards:write, pins:read and pins:write.
  • What it does: reads the account's username and board list, creates boards for our product categories, and creates pins showing our own product images with a title, description and a link to the matching Etsy listing. It may read basic pin metrics (such as saves and outbound clicks) for our own pins to see which products interest people.
  • What it does not do: it does not read, collect or store data about other Pinterest users, it does not follow, message, comment or save other people's pins, and it does not use Pinterest data for advertising or sell it to anyone.
  • Storage: the OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in a private, access-restricted file used only by our own automation, never in public code or shared with third parties. Pin metrics are kept only as summary numbers for our own shop and are not combined with any other data.
  • Deletion: the account owner can revoke access at any time in Pinterest settings (Security and logins > Apps). When access is revoked or the tool is retired, we delete the stored tokens and any stored Pinterest data.

Your rights

If you have bought from us and want to know what we hold about you, or want it corrected or deleted (where Etsy's records and the law allow), send us an Etsy message and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

Our products and this website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
